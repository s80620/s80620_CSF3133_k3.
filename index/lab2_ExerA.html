About The Event

Faculty Technology Day is a one-day event for student who want to learn about programmming, web development and technology careers. The event will feature keynote speeches, interactive workshops and networking opportunities throughout the day.

Speaker: Dr. Amier Yushaimy

Dr. Amier Yushaimy is a renowned expert in the field of technology and has been a keynote speaker at numerous conferences worldwide. He will be sharing his insights on the latest trends in technology and how students can prepare for successful careers in this ever-evolving industry and explain how students can improve their problem-solving skills and knowledge in programming and web development.

Activity: Interactive Workshop

The interactive workshop will provide students with hands-on experience in programming and web development. Participants will have the opportunity to work on real-world projects and learn from industry professionals.
